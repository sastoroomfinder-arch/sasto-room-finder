Who we are

Practical local property assistance

Sasto Room Finder Pvt. Ltd. is a Nepal-focused real estate and room-finding service. We help people discover property options based on location, budget and their individual requirements.

Our website connects customers with available property listings and provides simple ways to contact the relevant owner or our team through phone and WhatsApp.

We focus on Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur and aim to make property searching simpler and more convenient.
